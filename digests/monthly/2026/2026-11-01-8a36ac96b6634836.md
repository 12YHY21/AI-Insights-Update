- [Don't Forget What You Just Learned: Self-Directed Repair Turns Agent Mistakes Into Lasting Lessons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31846) — **仍属前沿** 7.0/10：Self-Direction通过从错误中提炼LoRA适配器实现Agent的持续学习，在多个基准上显著提升成功率。该方向与Phronix、EvoSyn一致，是Agent学习的重要前沿。
- [Claude has improved on a longstanding lower bound for the fraction of zeros of the Riemann zeta function that satisfy the Riemann hypothesis](https://www.anthropic.com/research/riemann-zeta) — **仍属前沿** 7.0/10：未发布Claude模型显著改进了黎曼猜想的相关界限，展示了AI在深度数学研究中的潜力。该成果经过专家验证，属于AI for Science的重要标志。
